❤ Health & Wellness Resources

Tip: Regular checkups help catch health issues early. Schedule yours today!

🌿 Healthy Living Tips

  • Drink at least 8 glasses of water per day
  • Aim for 30 minutes of moderate exercise most days
  • Eat a balanced diet with fruits and vegetables
  • Get 7–9 hours of sleep each night
  • Avoid smoking and limit alcohol consumption

💋 Preventive Care Reminders

  • Annual physical exam
  • Blood pressure check every 1–2 years
  • Cholesterol screening every 4–6 years (more often if at risk)
  • Flu shot every year
  • Dental cleaning every 6 months

📞 Emergency Contacts

  • Emergency: 911
  • Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222
  • Mental Health Crisis: 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
  • CarePoint Medical Center: (713) 555-0100
